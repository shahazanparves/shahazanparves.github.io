About

I use density functional theory to work out why a material behaves the way it does, and to predict which composition should behave better. The materials are solar absorbers, hydrogen storage hydrides, battery anodes and photocatalysts.

My background sits across disciplines: a BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering, then an MSc in Environmental Systems with a major in Resource and Chemical Systems. That mix is what draws me to composition-structure-property relationships in semiconductors, functional oxides, perovskites, alloys and nanomaterials, and to the parts of those relationships that electronic structure, defects and interfaces actually govern.

Most of my published work screens families of materials rather than single compounds, substituting one cation or halogen at a time and tracking how stability, band gap, optical response and transport move together. Alongside the electronic structure I run device-level simulations in SCAPS‑1D, so a predicted absorber can be carried through to a current-voltage curve. For my doctorate I want to close the remaining loop and pair these calculations with experimental synthesis and characterization.

Research focus

Five material families, one method

Lead-free double halide perovskites

Vacancy-ordered and elpasolite-type halides as non-toxic replacements for lead absorbers. Band alignment, optical absorption, thermoelectric response and lattice stability across the halogen series.

Cs₂B′B″X₆ · X₂NaIrCl₆ · M₂KIrCl₆ · Cs₂CuLuY₆

Hydride perovskites for hydrogen storage

Gravimetric capacity, formation energy and desorption behaviour of ternary hydrides, checked for dynamic stability with ab initio molecular dynamics.

MgBH₃ (B = Mo, In) · MgXH₃ (X = Ga, Tl)

Half-Heusler alloys and chalcogenides

Electronic, elastic and thermomechanical properties of ternary intermetallics and layered chalcogenides for optoelectronic and thermoelectric use.

ANiSn · V-based half-Heuslers · TlCrX₂ (X = S, Se)

Battery anodes and photocatalysts

Progressive lithiation of anatase TiO₂ with DFT+U, migration barriers from nudged elastic band calculations, and dopant-induced in-gap states that shift the absorption edge into the visible.

TiO₂ · LiTiO₂ · Li₂TiO₂ · Ag- and NaF-doped TiO₂

Correlated and magnetic oxides

Hubbard-corrected treatment of transition-metal oxides where on-site correlation decides the magnetic ground state and the spin character of the carriers.

Spinel LiTi₂O₄

Perovskite solar cell devices

Carrying a calculated absorber into a full device stack: band offsets, defect density, series resistance, and the quantum efficiency and impedance response that follow.

SCAPS‑1D · J-V · EQE · Mott-Schottky

Methods

What I actually run

Electronic structure
Geometry and variable-cell relaxation, convergence testing of k-point mesh and plane-wave cutoff, pseudopotential and exchange-correlation functional selection, band structure and projected density of states, DFT+U for correlated d states.
Dynamics and kinetics
Ab initio molecular dynamics for finite-temperature stability; nudged elastic band for migration barriers and diffusion pathways.
Charge and bonding
Bader charge analysis, charge-density difference maps, ion adsorption energetics, photocatalytic potential from band-edge positions.
Derived properties
Elastic constants and mechanical stability criteria, phonon and thermodynamic quantities, optical dielectric response, thermoelectric transport coefficients.
Device modelling
SCAPS‑1D thin-film and perovskite solar cells: architecture design, defect and interface parameters, J-V characteristics, quantum efficiency, Mott-Schottky and impedance analysis.
Codes
Quantum ESPRESSO, CASTEP, WIEN2k, Materials Studio, SCAPS‑1D, thermo_pw, VESTA, Origin, MATLAB; Linux workflows on HPC clusters.
